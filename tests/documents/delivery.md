# Delivery refunds policy

What customers get when a parcel is late, lost or damaged.

## Late deliveries

1. An express parcel more than 2 days late gets a refund if the customer asks.
2. Express customers get a $10 voucher for any delay.
3. A customer never gets both a refund and a voucher.

## Couriers

- Courier parcels are never refunded.
- A late courier parcel is refunded if the customer asks. Lost parcels are always refunded.

## Damaged and sale items

Damaged items are always replaced. Sale items can't be returned
or exchanged.
