# Late delivery compensation

This policy says what a customer gets when a parcel arrives late.

## When a parcel is late

1. A parcel is late if it arrives 2 or more days after the promised date, or 1 or more days late when a courier
   delivered it. Nothing else makes a parcel late.

## Compensation

2. A customer whose parcel is late gets a refund if they want their money back, and a voucher if they don't.
3. A customer whose parcel isn't late gets neither a refund nor a voucher.
4. A customer who gets a refund doesn't also get a voucher.

## About deliveries

A parcel is never delivered before the promised date. We publish delivery times on every product page.
