# Flight disruption and cancellation policy

This policy says what a customer is entitled to when their flight is disrupted, or when they cancel.

## Disruptions

1. A flight is significantly disrupted if and only if it is cancelled, or delayed by 3 hours or more.
2. A disruption is within the airline's control if and only if it is caused by crew, engineering, scheduling or IT
   systems.

## Choosing not to travel

3. If a flight is significantly disrupted for a reason within our control and the customer chooses not to travel,
   they get a full refund, whatever their fare.
4. If a flight is significantly disrupted for a reason outside our control and the customer chooses not to travel,
   a Flex fare gets a full refund. A Lite or Choice fare gets a travel credit for the full fare, unless the 24-hour
   rule gives a refund.

## Still travelling

5. If a flight is significantly disrupted for any reason and the customer still wants to travel, they get free
   rebooking.
6. Refunds and travel credits are only for customers who choose not to travel.
7. A customer who has flown is not entitled to a refund or travel credit for that flight.

## Cancelling

8. A cancellation is a 24-hour cancellation if and only if it is within 24 hours of booking and departure is more
   than 7 days away. A 24-hour cancellation gets a full refund, whatever the fare.
9. When a customer cancels a flight that is not significantly disrupted, a Flex fare gets a full refund, a Choice
   fare gets a travel credit, and a Lite fare gets neither a refund nor a travel credit.
10. A customer never receives both a refund and a travel credit for the same flight.

## Service

A delay is never negative. Our team will always explain the options before a booking is changed.
