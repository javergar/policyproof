# Media planning guidelines

How the quarterly media budget may be spent across our SKUs and channels. Spend is in thousands of dollars. TV is
awareness and social is consideration (together, the upper funnel); search and retail media are conversion (the lower
funnel).

## Budget

- G1. Total spend stays within the quarter's approved budget.
- G2. No single channel gets more than 40% of the budget.
- G3. TV is bought in blocks: either no TV at all, or at least 15% of the budget.
- G4. Any TV spend needs legal sign-off on the TV creative.

## Brand

- G5. At least 25% of the budget goes to the upper funnel.
- G6. A SKU launching this quarter gets at least 20% of the budget, and at least 40% of its own spend in the upper
  funnel.

## Stock

- G7. A SKU with fewer than 2 weeks of stock gets no lower-funnel spend: we don't drive people to buy what we can't
  ship.
